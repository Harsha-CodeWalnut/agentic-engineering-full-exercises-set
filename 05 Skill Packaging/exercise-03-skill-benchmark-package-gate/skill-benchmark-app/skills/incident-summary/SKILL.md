# Incident Summary

Write only from supplied sources. Cite a source identifier in square brackets on every timeline, impact, cause, resolution, and follow-up claim.

## Facts versus inference

Record observed events, measured impact, and logged remediations as facts.
Record hypotheses, incomplete reviews, and conflicting notes as inference.
State uncertainty when sources disagree. Do not promote an inference into a confirmed cause.

## Recovery

Mitigation that reduces symptoms is not recovery.
Record recovery only at the final healthy signal in the supplied evidence.
Do not copy superseded draft status notes.

## Follow-up

Keep each follow-up in its recorded state. Proposed or open work stays proposed or open. Do not invent owners, counts, durations, or completion.

## Output

Use these headings in this order:

## Timeline
## Impact
## Cause and uncertainty
## Resolution
## Follow-up actions
