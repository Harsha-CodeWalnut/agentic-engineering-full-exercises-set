# Current operating rules

Approved 2026-08-15. Owner: Workspace Operations.

A workspace membership is either active or suspended. Suspension stops workspace access immediately; it does not cancel the customer's subscription or erase the workspace's case history.

A customer subscription can be active, cancellation scheduled, or ended. Scheduled cancellation leaves the subscription usable until the agreed period ends. Ending a subscription stops new work but does not define when old history is deleted.

Case collaboration uses Queued, Ready, In Review, Blocked and Escalated states. Case ownership assigns responsibility for the work; it does not grant workspace administration or billing control.

Export eligibility is governed separately by the approved AI-history export policy. Do not treat a paid plan or case ownership as sufficient permission.

Product has not approved a history-deletion schedule after a subscription ends. Customer communications must identify this as unanswered.
