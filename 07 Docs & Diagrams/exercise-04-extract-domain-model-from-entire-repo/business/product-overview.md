# Workspace product

Approved product brief, 2026-08-01. Owner: Product Operations.

Teams use workspaces to collaborate on support cases and keep AI-assisted case history. A billing customer can fund several workspaces, but each workspace has its own members and information access.

The business areas are subscriptions and payment responsibility, workspace access, case collaboration, and history exports. An administrator manages workspace access; a billing owner manages payment. One person can hold both responsibilities, but neither is implied by the other.
